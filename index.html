About Baby Kedim

Baby Kedim is a cosy virtual pet game about a tiny black kitten with big green eyes. He needs four things: food, fun, a bath and sleep. Coloured rings on the room buttons show at a glance what he wants next.

Look after him across six rooms, play mini-games, grow crops in the garden and dress him up. He grows from a kitten into one of three grown-up cats, depending on which mini-games he plays most.

3 quick tips

  1. Start every visit with the daily gift, then fix the lowest need first.
  2. He only grows while all four needs are fairly full, so keep every ring topped up rather than maxing out one.
  3. Feed him crops from the garden instead of buying food, and save your coins for outfits.

How to play

🐾 Baby Kedim needs food, fun, a bath and sleep. The rings on the room buttons show how he feels.

🍴 Kitchen: drag a food to Kedim's mouth.

🛁 Bathroom: rub the soap all over him, then tap Rinse.

🌙 Bedroom: tap Lights off for a nap. He wakes up when rested.

🛋️ Living Room: pet him for purrs, throw the ball, talk to him, and play 6 mini-games for coins.

🌻 Garden: plant seeds, water them twice and harvest. Sell crops at the Farm Stand or feed them to your kitty. Every harvest grows your garden! Play Garden Ball too.

🎹 Hobby Room: play a tune on the piano, paint a picture for the wall, and write cute poems.

🐱 More cats: tap the kitty face at the top to adopt up to 3 cats, each with their own name, fur and needs.

🌱 A happy Kedim grows: Kitten → Kid → Teen → Adult. The mini-game he plays most decides if he becomes Therapist, Snack-Swing or Adventurer Kedim!

💛 Kedim never gets hurt. If you forget him he just gets sulky or messy and stops growing until you care for him.

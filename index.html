How to play

🐾 Baby Kedim needs food, fun, a bath and sleep. The rings on the room buttons show how he feels.

🍽️ Kitchen: drag a food to Kedim's mouth.

🛁 Bathroom: rub the soap all over him, then tap Rinse.

🌙 Bedroom: tap Lights off for a nap. He wakes up when rested.

🧶 Playroom: pet him for purrs, throw the ball, talk to him, and play 6 mini-games for coins.

🌱 A happy Kedim grows: Kitten → Kid → Teen → Adult. The mini-game he plays most decides if he becomes Therapist, Snack-Swing or Adventurer Kedim!

💛 Kedim never gets hurt. If you forget him he just gets sulky or messy and stops growing until you care for him.
